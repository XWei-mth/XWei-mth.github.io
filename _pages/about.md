I am currently a postdoctoral research fellow at the [ECOPRO](https://www.ibs.re.kr/ecopro/) group, [Institute for Basic Science](https://www.ibs.re.kr/eng.do). 

I received my Ph.D. in Applied Mathematics from the [School of Mathematical Science](https://math.ustc.edu.cn/), [University of Science and Technology of China](https://www.ustc.edu.cn/) in 2025, under the supervision of Prof. [Xiande Zhang](http://staff.ustc.edu.cn/~drzhangx/). During my doctoral studies, I was also a student member of the [Interdisciplinary Research Group of Combinatorics and Information Theory](http://staff.ustc.edu.cn/~drzhangx/group-chn/default.html), supervised by Prof. [Gennian Ge](https://math.cnu.edu.cn/szdw/qtjs/122195.htm). I obtained my B.Sc. degree in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from USTC in 2019.

My research interests lie in extremal graph theory, additive combinatorics, design theory, and algebraic methods, with applications to DNA-based communication channels.
